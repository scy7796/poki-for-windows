# What still needs the real phone

PokiCore's look/do is tested three ways: Linux and macOS unit tests against a fake WebDriverAgent
(fault injection included), and `poki-sim-check` against the real WebDriverAgent in the iOS
Simulator (CI job `simulator`). These are the parts only an iPhone can confirm.

| What | Why the simulator cannot settle it | How to check on the phone |
|---|---|---|
| `animationCoolOffTimeout` 0 / `waitForIdleTimeout` 0.5 make drags fast in apps that never stop animating | The simulator has no X or WeChat, and its timing differs | In X, `drag` and `swipe` take well under 1 s each and never time out |
| Swipes land on the list, not the reply or input bar, in X and WeChat | Their trees are not in the simulator | `swipe up` in an X reply thread and a WeChat chat: `screen_changed: true` |
| Hit-test: a visible control inside an input bar or keyboard is tapped at its centre; a post under X's reply bar is tapped at its free part, or at its visible centre with a note; Photos' Select over the grid is tapped at its centre with no note | Needs those apps' trees | Tap WeChat's Send by number; tap a post under X's reply bar by number; tap Select in Photos by number |
| The tree of X's For You feed comes within a read (4 s) with `treeFormat: "xml"` | The simulator has no X; the JSON tree's per-element custom-action fetch is the suspect, not proven on the phone | `look` on X's feed with the app built with the XML tree: `elements (…)` listed, read under 4 s |
| An action this side gave up on ends the session, and nothing queued behind it runs late; a read that times out keeps the session | The simulator's Settings never holds WDA long enough to queue actions behind a read | On X's feed, a `swipe` after a late tree answers `not_sent … still finishing` or runs, never minutes later; the Poki log shows no new session per failed look |
| Vision OCR inside `[collapsed]` cells on `full=true`, and of the whole screen when the tree is late | Tested on macOS with rendered text, not on an iPhone screenshot | `look full=true` in an X thread lists the like and reply counts as `Text` elements |
| `via: "paste"`: the edit menu's Paste item is found and tapped, and the owner's clipboard comes back only once the text has landed (iOS's "Allow Paste" prompt is the owner's to answer) | WDA's pasteboard on a device may need the runner in front (iOS privacy rules); the simulator shows no paste prompt | Copy something, `type` with `via: "paste"` into an app that has never pasted from the runner, allow the paste: the field gets the text, not the owner's clipboard, and the clipboard then holds the owner's text again |
| `/wda/locked` on a locked phone | The simulator is never locked by a passcode | Lock the phone, call `do`: `screen: locked`, nothing sent |
| Approval: the action runs after the owner approves late in the wait | The Live Activity's buttons are the app's | Ask for a deletion, approve after ~15 s: the action is sent and the read follows |
| Typing CJK through `/wda/keys` on the phone's keyboard | The simulator's keyboard is not the phone's (third-party keyboards, input methods) | Type `你好 Poki 123` into WeChat: the reply's `the field now reads` matches |
| A tap that opens Poki (the Live Activity in the Dynamic Island) is undone: the app comes back and the read is made again | The simulator has no Poki app and no Live Activity | In Mail, `do` `{tap: Back}` while the island is expanded: the answer starts `app: Mail` and carries `note: Poki was in front after the actions …; Mail was brought back` |
| A launch sent while the runner is starting gets its full 9 s | The CI runner is up before the checks start | Turn the session on, send `do` `{launch: com.apple.mobilemail}` at once: `launch com.apple.mobilemail (sent, …)`, Mail in front |
| A banner or a system alert no longer makes the tree a SpringBoard dump: the detection point at (200, 420) finds the app under the banner | The simulator shows no banners during the checks | Have a notification arrive while reading JD or WeChat: `app:` names the app, not the Home screen |
| A system alert in front is listed in the tree as SpringBoard's (`app: Home screen (SpringBoard: … a system alert)`), and its buttons can be tapped by number | The simulator checks raise no alerts | Trigger an "Allow Paste" or a location alert: `look` lists its buttons; `do` `{tap: n}` on Allow works |
| The picture retaken when the tree came over 0.6 s later matches the tree's screen | Settings on the simulator answers its tree at once | Open a chat from WeChat's list in one `do`: the picture shows the chat, as the elements do |
| A hand-off (`{handoff: {kind, text}}`) shows in the Live Activity and Done ends it | The simulator has no Poki app | Hand a step off from an agent, tap Done on the phone: the next answer says `handoff: done at …` |
| The depth back-off after a timed-out tree read (depth 25, then 50 again) keeps the tree readable on a dense screen | The CI gym's dense screen is the only big tree we have | On a long feed, a `look` after a timeout lists the elements to depth 25 and says so; the next fast read restores 50 |
| The first session's settings hold for sessionless reads for the runner's life | The simulator never restarts the runner mid-run | After the runner is restarted, the first `look` names the app under a banner, not the Home screen |
| The wedge watchdog restarts the runner after 15 s + 3 s and the launcher brings it back with the same port | Only the fake and the CI gym's hung screen; the phone never wedged for 15 s in a test | Hang an app on the phone (a debug build with a sleeping main thread): within 20 s `look` says `runner: the runner wedged on …, so it was restarted`, and the next `look` reads the screen |
| A `scroll_to` cut short at 6 s goes on from where it stopped when called again | The gym list is the only 200-row list tried | Scroll a long Settings list to its last row in two or three `scroll_to` calls; each says how many swipes it made |
| A WKWebView's page parts come into the tree on the phone (the gym's never did), and the shallow re-read after a timeout answers on a real feed | The simulator's WebContent process never listed its page; the phone was not tried | Open a web page in Safari on the phone: `look` lists links and headings inside the WebView, or the WebView [collapsed] with the picture's text; a long feed `look` says "read again to depth 25 within this call" instead of no tree |
| A keyboard element is in the tree whenever a field is focused on the phone (the focus wait and the "no field was focused" note rest on it) | The simulator listed it in every run; a phone with a hardware keyboard paired, or a custom keyboard, was not tried | Type into Notes with a Bluetooth keyboard connected: the note says no keyboard came up although the text landed |
| The app recorded at a task's first call is the app the owner asked from (Grok Bot), also when that call had to start a sleeping runner | Starting WDA on the phone may bring the runner or the home screen to the front before the first read; the simulator checks start with the runner up | Let the runner fall asleep, ask in Grok Bot "打开天气看看明天温度": the phone goes to Weather and comes back to Grok Bot, not to the home screen |
| `apps/activate` of the app the task began in lands where the owner left it (Grok Bot's chat with the reply, Poki's chat page) | Third-party apps and Poki's chat are not in the simulator | After the Grok Bot task above, the reply is on screen without a tap |
